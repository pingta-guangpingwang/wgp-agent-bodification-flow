# Community code of conduct / 社区行为准则

We are committed to a respectful, accessible, and technically honest community. 我们致力于建设尊重他人、方便参与并对技术主张保持诚实的社区。

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- welcome beginners and explain specialized terms;
- disclose uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and material AI assistance;
- respect privacy, copyright, licenses, and attribution;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption.

参与者应讨论观点和证据，而非攻击个人；欢迎新手并解释术语；披露不确定性、利益冲突和实质性的 AI 协助；尊重隐私、著作权、许可与署名；不得骚扰、歧视、威胁、公开他人隐私、施加性关注或蓄意破坏讨论。

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing a reporter or affected person.

Until a private project contact is published, do not include sensitive personal details in a public report. Use GitHub's private security-reporting feature for security matters; for conduct matters, contact the repository owner privately through the account linked to this project.
